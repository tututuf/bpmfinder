Result meanings
BPM/key: full-recording onset/chroma estimates; confirm by ear.
Key retains its best-ranked profile when related keys are close; broad noise
and near-uniform pitch distributions supply no key. Resonant percussion can
yield an approximate pitch-profile candidate without a composed tonal center.
Camelot is derived from the selected key. DSP policy version: dsp-3.
Energy: RMS level mapped from -60..0 dBFS to 0..100, not musical intensity.
Dance/Happy: relative CLAP audio/text similarity to balanced description
ensembles, sigmoid temperature 0.07. These are custom zero-shot match scores,
not calibrated class probabilities or scores from another service.
CLAP analyzes 10-second windows through the complete recording, with
remaining-duration weighting for the overlapping last window. Short audio is
repeat-padded deterministically. Silent windows provide no mood evidence.
No arbitrary mood fallback is inferred from BPM, loudness or key.

Audio/results remain in browser memory. Model/runtime cache contains no audio.
The separate legacy backup is disabled by default and is not covered by the
licenses of this default engine. Its original restrictions remain applicable.
