# bpm-finder.net advertising revenue scenarios

Research date: 2026-10-30. These are advertising-only gross-revenue scenarios, not observed publisher earnings or net profit.

## Observed monetization configuration

Direct HTTP retrieval of https://bpm-finder.net/ returned 200 and contained an AdSense loader (`pagead2.googlesyndication.com/pagead/js/adsbygoogle`) and a configured publisher identifier. https://bpm-finder.net/ads.txt returned 200 and a Google DIRECT publisher entry. This establishes configuration only, not successful ad serving, ad visibility or any revenue.

## Traffic input and confidence

New Site Radar's public domain report at https://newsiteradar.com/es/site/bpm-finder.net was retrieved successfully. It labels its traffic figures as third-party estimates, with a 2026-09 snapshot and last synchronization 2026-10-05. Reported monthly visits: 108,649; pages per visit: 1.6; average duration: 19 seconds. Reported geography: US15.8%, Germany7.3%, UK5.5%, Russia2.9%, Vietnam2.8%. US+Germany+UK total28.6%; this is not the total of all valuable advertising geographies. The report's separate AI pricing/revenue interpretation includes unrelated ShipAny material and was excluded. No independent publisher Analytics, Search Console or AdSense access was available, and these traffic numbers were not corroborated against an independent measurement.

Implied monthly page views: 108,649 * 1.6 = 173,838.4, approximately174,000. Visits are not unique visitors and not ad impressions. The pages/visit input is rounded, so the implied PV figure must not be treated as precise.

## Scenario arithmetic

Google's Page RPM definition: estimated earnings / page views *1000. Source: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/112030?hl=en . Therefore revenue = PV /1000 *PageRPM. Page RPM already accounts for the combined revenue from ads on a page; do not multiply again by ad-unit count.

These RPM values are analyst-selected scenarios, not measured domain RPM, AdSense quotes or established category benchmarks. Working planning band: roughly USD200-700/month at the reported traffic; an optimistic USD1000 scenario needs realized RPM nearUSD5.75. A USD3000/month advertising target atRPM2-4 requires about750,000-1,500,000PV/month, approximately469,000-938,000visits at1.6pages/visit.

Google explicitly lists advertiser demand, user location/device, content vertical, seasonality and ad format/size/count as variables: https://adsense.google.com/start/ . Short reported visits and low pages/visit may limit viewability/inventory, but no causal revenue effect was measured. Ad blockers, consent, eligible traffic and ad serving can lower realized RPM; their actual rates are unknown here.

## Business interpretation

At this traffic estimate, ads could be supplementary income. The evidence does not support claiming thousands of dollars per month in actual receipts. Costs, taxes and subscription/affiliate income are excluded. Browser-local audio processing can reduce analysis-server cost in our project, but this report did not audit this competitor's complete infrastructure or costs.
